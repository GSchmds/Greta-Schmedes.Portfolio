Medal Holder

A wall display for an athlete's medals that shows where they come from and what they compete in. Designed in Fusion 360 and cut on a laser cutter.

Type
E-portfolio project, Dubai, UAE, 09.2021 to 04.2022
Client
A German athlete: hurdles, high jump, long jump
My role
Design, CAD, laser cutting, assembly, testing
Tools
Autodesk Fusion 360, Tech-soft 2D Design, laser cutter
Result
55 × 27.5 × 0.9 cm poplar board, 12 bars, holds 72 medals
Brief

A rack that says something

The client's old holder was a plain metal rack with hooks. Nothing on it said anything about them, and the medals hung on top of each other so most were hidden. They wanted something that shows their country and their sport, and holds every medal at once.

Holds
At least 80 medals
Shows
Country, sport and colours linked to the culture
Material
Wood, renewable, natural look
Safety
Smooth edges, hangs steadily on a wall
Time
Finished within three weeks

The audience is athletes who train and compete a lot. Athletics suits the project because it is an individual sport with many events, and every medal is something the athlete earned alone.

Try it

Hang the medals

Pick an event, then tap a bar to hang a medal. Each of the 12 bars holds six. The client sorted medals by event, one group per bar, which is why the bars are separate.

0 / 72

Nothing hung yet. The example puts one event on each row, so you can see how the board sorts medals.

The real load test

The client hung all 70 medals. The board held the weight and the layout let them group medals by event. The top and bottom rows showed. The middle row sat behind the ribbons from the row above, so the bars need more space between them.
