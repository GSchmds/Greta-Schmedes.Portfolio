Frisian Link

A safe website where students share projects about Frisian culture, take quizzes and compete as classes. The slogan is Be proud, be Frisian.

Type
University project, Buitenpost and Leeuwarden, 04.2025 to 06.2025
Client
Aeres VMBO Leeuwarden, through a Social Domain workshop
My role
Team captain, interface design in Figma, front-end coding in HTML and CSS
Tools
Figma, HTML, CSS, Miro, Coda, MoSCoW
Result
Working website: pinboard home, quiz, upload, challenges and class leaderboard
Brief

Pride without the risk

Aeres VMBO wanted a place where students aged 12 to 16 share photos, art, poems, music and videos about Frisian culture. It had to be safe from cyberbullying, easy to use and, at first, open only to the school. The school was being renovated, so we could not meet students.

Audience
Students aged 12 to 16
Goal
Pride in Frisian culture
Safety
No cyberbullying
Access
School only, at first
Time
One study period, seven weeks

We combined a youth survey at a Social Domain workshop with desk research on Frisian culture, teenage behaviour online and educational design. I was team captain, and we met in person every Tuesday and Thursday.
