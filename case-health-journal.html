Brief

A journal that does not judge

After bariatric surgery, small habits like drinking with meals or eating too fast can cause pain and setbacks. Frisius MC wanted a journal patients can fill in without stress. Many patients are between 40 and 75, often with lower education, and there is a real risk of low literacy.

Audience
Bariatric patients, mostly 40 to 75 years old
Reading level
Plain language, low-literacy risk
Habits
Wait 30 minutes between food and drink, chew 20 to 30 times, no fizzy drinks
Tone
Supportive and reassuring, not scary
First goal
Noticing habits, not changing everything at once

Patients cannot change everything at once, so the first journal, Noticing the habit, helps them see how mood, eating and body reactions connect. The series has three journals, each with its own colour theme.

Design

Five versions, one daily page

I went from sketch to final in about nine weeks. The client reacted to the first prototype and a patient reviewer reacted to the second.
